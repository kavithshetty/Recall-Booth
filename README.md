# Recall Booth

Listen, write it down, and see exactly what you missed. Recall Booth reads a
line of text aloud using your browser's built-in speech synthesis, you type
what you heard, and it scores your answer word-by-word against the original —
highlighting what you got right, what you missed, and what you added.

Built for spaced-repetition style listening practice: vocabulary, dictation,
memorizing passages, or just training your ear.

## Features
- Text-to-speech playback with adjustable speed and voice
- Word-level diff scoring (not just pass/fail)
- Paste in your own text — auto-splits into sentences or manual chunks
- Multiple saved sets, stored locally in the browser
- Weak lines automatically resurface at the end of a round
- No build step, no server, no
